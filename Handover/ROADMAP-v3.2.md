# Bull Roadmap After v3.2

## Layla Transport and Alert Ownership

Complete the jointly signed transport and decide which process owns wake-relative alerts
while Bull is suspended and already inside Home. Validate on a locked physical iPhone;
an App Group write alone is not a wake mechanism.

## Outside-Day Planning

Turn effective Home boundaries into a small day plan: leave-by time after final wake,
appointments/errands that keep the user out of risk zones, return-at wind-down time and a
clear weekend fallback. Keep suggestions editable and avoid treating an unplanned day as
a moral failure.

## Sleep Model Calibration

Keep the v3.2 purpose weights as versioned priors until enough personal outcomes exist.
Compare them prospectively without silently tuning weights. Consider sleep stages only
when source coverage and missing-stage behavior are explicit.

## Outcome Windows

Explore a bounded trigger→lapse interval that can cross midnight without assigning every
later lapse to an earlier trigger. Preserve the current same-day definition until a new
window is predeclared and versioned.

## Existing Candidates

Location-aware stress activities, stressful-event coaching, strength progression and
post-lapse recovery remain candidates from the v3.1 roadmap. Maintain the same evidence,
privacy and no-silent-reweighting guardrails.
